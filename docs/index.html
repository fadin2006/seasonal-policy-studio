Seasonal Policy Studio

Track a seasonal stochastic system, design a decision-tree policy, and see at once how far it is from the best possible policy.

Solver back end: checking

Research prototype. The built-in case takes its eight seasonal splits, 24 joint demand, wind and solar states, probabilities and fleet sizes from the scenario data of Reliable low-carbon power supply for cities: a deliverability-aware stochastic generation expansion approach (Muna, Malik and Syed, submitted to Smart Cities, 3 November 2026). The maintenance decision, the stressed environments and all costs are assumptions of a single-node study case, not results of the expansion model.

M Fahrudin Muna, King Fahd University of Petroleum and Minerals. Costs are expected unserved energy in GWh per year plus a charge for unfinished maintenance.
